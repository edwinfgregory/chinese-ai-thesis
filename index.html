Chinese perspectives on development, diffusion, and potential of artificial intelligence 中国对人工智能发展、扩散与潜力的认知

Edwin Farrell Gregory

Background 背景

Artificial intelligence is widely recognized as a technology with the potential to fundamentally reshape how humans relate to knowledge and material production.

人工智能被广泛认为是一种具有根本性变革潜力的技术，有望从根本上重塑人类与知识和物质生产的关系。

Given the rising cost of maintaining frontier capabilities, it increasingly seems that only two countries will be capable of competing in global artificial intelligence: China and America. The continual American fault of assuming that the Chinese have the same beliefs as us, a tendency known as mirroring, leads many technologists and policymakers to believe that the Chinese tend to hold similar assumptions around policy, trade-offs, and development. Given the stakes, it is all the more important to understand Chinese discourse around artificial intelligence.

随着前沿能力维护成本的持续上升，越来越多的迹象表明，只有两个国家能够在全球人工智能领域一较高下：中国与美国。美国长期存在一种将自身思维方式投射到中国身上的倾向，即"镜像化"谬误，这使许多技术专家和政策制定者误以为中国在政策取向、权衡考量和发展路径上与美国类似。正因如此，理解中国围绕人工智能的话语体系显得尤为重要。

Primary Research Question 主要研究问题

What are the mainstream Chinese perspectives on the development and diffusion of artificial intelligence?

中国对人工智能发展与扩散的主流观点是什么？

Individuals 研究对象

To populate the range of mainstream thought categories, I am examining the writings and statements of prominent scholars, technologists, and business leaders whose views are particularly relevant for trying to understand Chinese AI discourse. This list is certainly not all-encompassing, and I welcome any suggested additions.
