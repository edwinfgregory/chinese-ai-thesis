Beyond the AI Race

Understanding Chinese Visions for Artificial Intelligence

Most Western analysis assumes China is in a technological race for superintelligence. My thesis argues that this paradigm ignores the manifold Chinese perspectives on the future and potential of AI. As part of my thesis, I am researching the various ideas about artificial intelligence development and diffusion in China. You can read my initial findings in my thesis proposal.

I am currently working on primary source analysis and am seeking interviews to inform my research on Chinese AI perspectives.

Research Questions

Primary: What are the various Chinese perspectives on the potential of artificial intelligence development, and how do they inform policy and technological strategy?

  • Discourse Categorization: What are the major, competing schools of thought among leading Chinese AI researchers and policymakers?
  • Policy Validation: How does the Chinese AI+ Plan reflect this distinct, practical vision compared to the US focus on a "race to dominance"?
  • Embodied AI Viability: What are the long-term technical and economic challenges of a "physical-first" EAI strategy for China?
  • Distinctions within Embodied AI: What are the major disagreements between proponents of embodied AI?
  • Alternatives to Embodied AI: What are other non-LLM scaling perspectives critical of Embodied AI?
  • AGI Skeptics: What are the different perspectives within broader AGI skepticism? Has this skepticism grown stronger or weaker?
  • Sector Distinctions: Are there meaningful distinctions between perspectives of party officials, researchers, and the private sector?
  • Public Opinion: What are popular Chinese perspectives on AI development, and how have these changed over time?

中文摘要

大多数西方分析认为中国正在进行一场通用人工智能的技术竞赛。我的论文认为，这种范式忽略了中国对人工智能未来和潜力的多元视角。我正在研究中国关于人工智能发展和传播的各种观点，并寻求访谈机会以深入了解中国的人工智能视角。
