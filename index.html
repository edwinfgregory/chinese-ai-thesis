Primary Research Question

What are the various Chinese perspectives on the potential of artificial intelligence development, and how do they inform policy and technological strategy?

中国对人工智能发展潜力有哪些不同的观点，这些观点如何影响政策和技术战略？

Secondary Questions

次要问题

  • What are the major, competing schools of thought among leading Chinese AI researchers and policymakers? 中国主要人工智能研究人员和决策者之间有哪些主要的、相互竞争的思想流派？
  • How does the Chinese AI+ Plan reflect a distinct, practical vision compared to the US focus on a "race to dominance"? 与美国关注"竞争主导权"相比，中国的AI+计划如何体现这种独特的务实愿景？
  • What are the long-term technical and economic challenges of a "physical-first" embodied AI strategy for China? 中国"物理优先"具身人工智能战略面临哪些长期技术和经济挑战？
  • What are the major disagreements between proponents of embodied AI? 具身人工智能支持者之间有哪些主要分歧？
  • What non-LLM-scaling perspectives exist that are also critical of embodied AI? 还有哪些非大语言模型扩展的观点对具身人工智能持批评态度？
  • What are the different perspectives within broader AGI skepticism in China? 在中国更广泛的通用人工智能怀疑论中有哪些不同观点？
  • Are there meaningful distinctions between the views of party officials, researchers, and the private sector? 党政官员、研究人员和私营部门的观点之间是否存在有意义的区别？
  • How have popular Chinese perspectives on AI development changed over time? 中国公众对人工智能发展的看法随时间如何变化？
