Beyond the AI Race 超越人工智能竞赛

Understanding Chinese Visions for Artificial Intelligence 理解中国对人工智能的愿景

Most Western analysis assumes China is in a technological race for superintelligence. My thesis argues that this paradigm ignores the manifold Chinese perspectives on the future and potential of AI. As part of my thesis, I am researching the various ideas about artificial intelligence development and diffusion in China. You can read my initial findings in my thesis proposal.

大多数西方分析认为中国正在进行一场通用人工智能的技术竞赛。我的论文认为，这种范式忽略了中国对人工智能未来和潜力的多元视角。作为论文研究的一部分，我正在研究中国关于人工智能发展和传播的各种观点。您可以在我的论文提案中阅读我的初步发现。

Research Questions 研究问题

Primary 主要问题

What are the various Chinese perspectives on the potential of artificial intelligence development, and how do they inform policy and technological strategy?

中国对人工智能发展潜力有哪些不同的观点，这些观点如何影响政策和技术战略？

Secondary 次要问题

  • What are the major, competing schools of thought among leading Chinese AI researchers and policymakers?
  • How does the Chinese AI+ Plan reflect this distinct, practical vision compared to the US focus on a "race to dominance"?
  • What are the long-term technical and economic challenges of a "physical-first" embodied AI strategy for China?
  • What are the major disagreements between proponents of embodied AI?
  • What are other non-LLM scaling perspectives critical of Embodied AI?
  • What are the different perspectives within broader AGI skepticism?
  • Are there meaningful distinctions between perspectives of party officials, researchers, and the private sector?
  • What are popular Chinese perspectives on AI development, and how have these changed over time?
  • 中国主要人工智能研究人员和决策者之间有哪些主要的、相互竞争的思想流派？
  • 与美国关注"竞争主导权"相比，中国的AI+计划如何体现这种独特的务实愿景？
  • 中国"物理优先"具身人工智能战略面临哪些长期技术和经济挑战？
  • 具身人工智能支持者之间有哪些主要分歧？
  • 还有哪些非大语言模型扩展的观点对具身人工智能持批评态度？
  • 在更广泛的通用人工智能怀疑论中有哪些不同观点？
  • 党政官员、研究人员和私营部门的观点之间是否存在有意义的区别？
  • 中国公众对人工智能发展有哪些看法，这些看法随时间如何变化？
